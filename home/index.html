Overview · Research · In progress

Quantum Kernels for EEG

Can a quantum kernel help a support vector machine tell EEG signals apart?

The problem: EEG signals are noisy and hard to separate.

Brain signals carry a lot of noise and change from person to person, so a classifier needs a good way to measure how alike two recordings are.

The approach: let a quantum circuit measure similarity.

A quantum kernel encodes each signal as a quantum state and measures how much two states overlap. A support vector machine then uses that overlap as its kernel.
